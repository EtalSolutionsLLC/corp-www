# ReviewNudge Is Coming to iPhone, Bringing Customer Review Follow-Up Into the Field

The upcoming iOS app puts customer outreach, follow-up status, and review progress where the work happens.

LONG BEACH, Calif. - Aug. 3, 2026 - PRLog -- Et al Solutions LLC today announced that ReviewNudge is coming to iPhone in August 2026

ReviewNudge gives service businesses one organized place to add customers, send thoughtful review requests, track follow-ups, and see where each customer stands. The iPhone app brings that same focused workflow closer to the moment a job is finished—when the work is fresh and asking for feedback makes the most sense.

The mobile experience is designed for real working days: fewer steps, clear status, and the information a business owner or team member needs while moving between customers. It extends ReviewNudge beyond the desk without turning a straightforward review request into another complicated system.

The ReviewNudge iOS app remains subject to Apple's review process. Et al Solutions is targeting App Store availability in August 2026 and will publish confirmed availability as soon as the listing is live.

Learn more at [ReviewNudge](https://reviewnudge.etal.solutions/) or follow company announcements at [Et al Solutions](https://www.etal.solutions/).

**About ReviewNudge**

ReviewNudge is a customer-review workspace from Et al Solutions LLC. It helps service businesses organize customer outreach, follow-up activity, review status, and team visibility in one straightforward place.
