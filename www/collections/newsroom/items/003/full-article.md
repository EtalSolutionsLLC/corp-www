# ReviewNudge Comes to iPhone, Bringing Customer Review Follow-Up Into the Field

LONG BEACH, Calif. - Aug. 23, 2026 - PRLog -- Et al Solutions LLC today announced that ReviewNudge will be available on iPhone through a mobile-optimized web experience beginning August 24, 2026.

ReviewNudge gives service businesses one organized place to add customers, send review requests, track follow-ups, and monitor review progress. The new iPhone experience brings those capabilities closer to the moment a job is completed, making it easier to manage customer outreach while still in the field.

Designed specifically for mobile devices rather than adapted from a desktop layout, the experience focuses on speed, clarity, and ease of use. Business owners and team members can quickly review customer status, send requests, and stay on top of follow-ups without returning to a desk.

"Review requests are most effective when they happen close to the customer interaction," said David Omer, founder of Et al Solutions LLC. "The new mobile experience helps businesses act while the work is still fresh in the customer's mind."

**About ReviewNudge**

ReviewNudge is a customer-review management platform from Et al Solutions LLC. It helps service businesses organize customer outreach, manage follow-ups, and track review activity in one straightforward workspace accessible from desktop and mobile devices.
